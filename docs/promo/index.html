Long description
OpenHands Mobile - your AI coding agents, in your pocket.

Ever wanted to check on an agent run while you are away from your desk? OpenHands Mobile brings your AI coding agents to Android. Launch runs, watch what they are doing, and approve actions, all from your phone.

It is a native Android shell around the OpenHands Agent Canvas. Connect it to an OpenHands server on your laptop, a VPS, or the cloud, and your whole workflow fits in your pocket.

The screenshots are real captures of the app, not mockups. First launch walks you through four steps: connect to your server, pick an agent, choose a model, then send your first message or open a ready-made workflow.

Why you will like it:
- Supervise agents remotely, no laptop needed
- Lightweight: 6 MB download, about 12 MB installed
- Asks for internet access and nothing else
- Clean updates that install straight over the old version
- Free and open source under the MIT licence
- Works on Android 6.0 and newer

Posting notes

  • Links are not clickable in Instagram captions. Write the link as plain text, drop it in the first comment too, and add "link in bio" if you can.
  • Use the carousel, not one image. Carousels usually reach further than a single post.
  • Keep hashtags between 8 and 12. More than that reads as spam.
  • Never crop the images yourself. They are already sized and centre-safe. Cropping them again is what caused the problem before.
